Create your first cluster:

  kubectl apply -f - <<'YAML'
  apiVersion: objectstorage.openeverest.io/v1alpha1
  kind: ObjectStorageCluster
  metadata:
    name: quickstart
  spec:
    version: "3.80"
    volume:
      replicas: 1
      storage:
        size: 5Gi
  YAML

Watch it come up:

  kubectl get objectstoragecluster quickstart -w

The S3 endpoint appears on .status.s3Endpoint once the cluster is ready. The
operator's own admin credentials live in the Secret named by
.status.adminSecretName; create an ObjectStorageUser rather than handing those
out to workloads.
